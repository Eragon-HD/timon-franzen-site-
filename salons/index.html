The scope

What is included

A clear home for your salon, your services, and the details clients need before they book.

  • Home page introducing the salon, owner, and photos
  • Services with prices and appointment durations
  • Your real Google reviews
  • Links to your booking tool, phone, and email
  • An update bar for holidays and offers
  • Fast loading and designed for mobile
  • Structured business details for Google and AI assistants
  • Imprint and privacy policy pages

Clear pricing

One price to launch. One clear monthly fee.

Website, one-time CHF 2,900 Care and maintenance: CHF 99/month
First 3 salons CHF 1,500 one-time, then CHF 99/month

Monthly care includes website and domain monitoring, backups, and up to two content changes. Minimum term: 12 months. After that, cancel with three months' notice at the end of a month.

The domain stays in your name and is paid directly by you. A booking system is not included; we link your existing one. You provide photos. Professional photography is available on request.

How we work

  1. Confirm the project

    You approve the offer. Half of the one-time fee is due to start.

  2. Send your content

    Share photos, prices, services, and a few words about the salon. Your domain stays yours.

  3. Review the first draft

    The first draft arrives within five working days. Two rounds of revisions are included.

  4. Approve and go live

    After approval and the remaining payment, the site goes live within seven working days of receiving all content.
